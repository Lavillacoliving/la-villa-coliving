La Villa Coliving — the essentials

La Villa Coliving is 3 coliving houses on the Geneva border, French side: La Villa in Ville-la-Grand (10 rooms), Le Loft in Ambilly (7 rooms) and Le Lodge in Annemasse (12 rooms) — 29 private furnished rooms of 16 to 24 m², with a pool, sauna and gym in every house, 20 minutes door-to-door from Geneva city centre.

  • All-inclusive rent from CHF 1,370/month (contractual rent in euros: from €1,470) — utilities, fibre up to 8 Gb/s, common-area cleaning 3 times a week, pool, sauna, gym, streaming, yoga and events included.

  • Who it's for: cross-border workers, expats and young professionals working in Geneva. Founded in October 2021 by Jérôme Austin and Fanny Bela and run directly by them — 100+ residents welcomed.
