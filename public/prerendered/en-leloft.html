About Le Loft

Le Loft is our most intimate house: 7 residents in a 300 m² townhouse in Ambilly, 500 m from the Swiss border. Its year-round heated indoor pool — virtually unique in European coliving — is the centerpiece of this exceptional property. The Finnish sauna, fully equipped gym, designer interiors, outdoor kitchen with TV, and spacious terraces make Le Loft ideal for those who appreciate the finer things while valuing genuine community. It's also a house where every room has its own private shower room, at CHF 1,430 all-inclusive.

Frequently asked questions

What is the monthly rent at Le Loft and what does it include?
Rooms at Le Loft are CHF 1,430 per month all-inclusive: utilities, fiber internet up to 8 Gb/s, common-area cleaning three times a week, streaming subscriptions, indoor pool, garden, bedding included. No hidden fees.
How do I get to Geneva from Le Loft in Ambilly?
Le Loft is 5 min walk from the Moillesulaz border and 5 min walk from TPG Tram 17 (Lancy-Pont-Rouge ↔ Annemasse). Central Geneva: 20 min via Tram 17. Secure bike paths to central Geneva: 25 min by bike.
What is the minimum lease term at Le Loft?
Flexible leases from 1 to 12 months. The default is 12 months, but shorter stays are accepted depending on availability — useful for cross-border workers on assignment or CDI trial periods in Geneva.
Is there a deposit and any agency fees?
Deposit equivalent to 2 months' rent excluding charges, refunded within 30 days after the move-out inspection. No agency fees, no application fees.
How many rooms are there at Le Loft and are they furnished?
7 private furnished rooms (bed, desk, wardrobe), all with a private en-suite bathroom. Designer common spaces: open kitchen, living room, terrace, year-round heated indoor pool.
Who can apply to live at Le Loft?
Target profile: cross-border workers on CDI, young professionals, expats. Selection by application (income proof, motivation, fit with community). The immediate proximity to the border makes Le Loft a favorite among cross-border workers who walk or bike to the office.
Where is Le Loft and how far from Geneva?
Le Loft is in Ambilly, on the French side, 20 minutes from Geneva city center by tram. It's one of the three La Villa Coliving houses, with an indoor pool heated year-round and a Finnish sauna.
How many residents live at Le Loft?
Le Loft hosts 7 residents, making it the most intimate of the La Villa Coliving houses. Located in Ambilly, near Geneva, it offers a private furnished room for each resident and a very convivial small-scale atmosphere.
What amenities are there at Le Loft?
Le Loft, in Ambilly, has an indoor pool heated and usable year-round, a Finnish sauna, a gym, a home cinema space and spacious rooms of 20 to 23 m². Everything is included in the all-inclusive rent of CHF 1,430/month.
Can you swim year-round at Le Loft?
Yes. Le Loft, in Ambilly, is the La Villa Coliving house with an indoor heated pool, accessible year-round whatever the season, plus a Finnish sauna. It's included in your rent, like all services.
