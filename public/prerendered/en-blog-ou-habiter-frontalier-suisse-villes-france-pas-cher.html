French Towns Near Geneva: 7 Best Places to Live (2026)

Jérôme and Fanny· co-founders of La Villa ColivingApril 5, 2026· Updated November 2, 202613 min

"I start at the HUG in early January, I don't know anyone in the area, and every colleague gives me a different town. Annemasse, Ferney, Saint-Julien… which one do I pick?" We get this message, give or take a few words, several times a month. So here is the answer, once and for all: seven towns on the French side to live in when you work in Geneva, compared with dated, sourced figures rather than adjectives. If you are arriving with a Geneva contract and a budget to keep, this is for you.

In short

  • The right town is not the one closest to the customs post, it is the one that puts your workplace under 30 minutes door to door with no car.
  • Left bank and city centre: Annemasse, Ville-la-Grand, Ambilly (Léman Express, tram 17). CERN, airport, international organisations: Ferney-Voltaire and Saint-Genis-Pouilly. Budget and lake: Thonon.
  • Wherever you live on the French side, your Geneva salary is taxed at source in Geneva: the town changes your rent and your commute, not your taxes.

How we ranked these towns

Four criteria, the same for every town, and the sources are in the text.

  1. Door-to-door commute, not kilometres. Read on Google Maps on a Monday, arriving at 8:30, from the town hall to a named Geneva station or stop, by public transport. No driving times: the border crossing at rush hour looks nothing like any displayed time.
  2. The official asking rent. The French Housing Ministry's Carte des loyers (2025 edition, ANIL) gives, town by town, a rent per m² including charges for an unfurnished one- or two-room flat put on the market in the third quarter of 2025: same source everywhere, so comparable.
  3. Life without a car. A Léman Express station or a tram 17 stop within walking distance: no customs queue, no parking in Geneva.
  4. What you do in the evening. A lively centre, a market, a lake, or a dormitory town: we say which.

More than 116,000 cross-border workers work in the canton of Geneva (OCSTAT, end of 2025), and most of them picked their town on a colleague's tip. You have the numbers.

If you would rather start from your profile than from the map, where you work in Geneva, car or no car, budget and length of stay, read our guide “Where to live in France when you work in Geneva”: it works profile by profile, while this one compares town by town.

1. Annemasse: the hub town, 7 minutes from Geneva Eaux-Vives by Léman Express

Annemasse is the only real town on the border: 37,600 inhabitants (INSEE 2023), a Léman Express station, a cross-border tram, a market, restaurants, a cinema. From the station, Geneva Eaux-Vives is 7 minutes away and Cornavin 23, with a train every 10 minutes at rush hour. One- or two-room asking rent: €22.6/m², about €840 for 37 m². Tram 17 gains three stops in the town at the end of 2026 (Annemasse Agglo).

In Annemasse itself, Le Lodge by La Villa Coliving (12 rooms, Romagny district) is a 10-minute walk from the station — Geneva Eaux-Vives in 18 minutes door to door.

For whom? People who want to go out on foot and catch the train without thinking about it. To pick your district: our full guide to Annemasse and the district-by-district comparison.

2. Ville-la-Grand: quiet, with the lowest rent in the corridor

Next door to Annemasse, residential, bordered by the Foron, the border river. No station: you walk to Annemasse's. One- or two-room asking rent: €17.1/m², about €630 for 37 m², the lowest of the seven towns in this guide. Studio listings in 2026 sit well above that indicator (see our Observatory): a tight market, not out of reach.

In Ville-la-Grand, La Villa by La Villa Coliving (10 rooms) is a 14-minute walk from Annemasse station — Geneva Eaux-Vives in 22 minutes door to door.

For whom? Couples, families, and anyone who wants a garden at French prices with the train a quarter of an hour away on foot.

3. Ambilly: the border on foot and tram 17

Ambilly touches the border: from Le Loft, the Foron is 600 m away, an 8-minute walk, and the Moillesulaz crossing 1.8 km. Tram 17 runs into Geneva with no change (Rive in 23 minutes from the Croix-d'Ambilly stop) and Annemasse station is an 18-minute walk. One- or two-room asking rent: €24.5/m², about €910 for 37 m²: proximity has a price.

In Ambilly, Le Loft by La Villa Coliving (7 rooms) is an 8-minute walk from tram 17 — Geneva Eaux-Vives in 24 minutes door to door.

For whom? Car-free workers on the left bank or in the centre, and cyclists: the Voie Verte runs straight to Rive.

4. Saint-Julien-en-Genevois: the south, for Plainpalais, Carouge and the HUG

A small town at the foot of the Salève, with a shopping centre and genuine local life. The railway station is marginal: what matters is the direct cross-border TPG bus, which puts Bel-Air, in central Geneva, 45 minutes away door to door from the town hall (Monday, 8:30). The extension of tram 15 to the station is stalled: the Federal Administrative Court annulled the approval of the second section in July 2026. One- or two-room asking rent: €23.8/m², about €880 for 37 m².

For whom? Anyone working in Plainpalais, Carouge, Lancy or at the HUG who accepts the bus while waiting for the tram.

5. Ferney-Voltaire: the gateway to the international organisations

In the Ain, next to the airport and the Nations district. A direct TPG bus puts Cornavin about forty minutes away door to door from the town hall (Monday, 8:30). A pleasant historic centre, a Saturday market, many English speakers. The downside: the highest rent in this guide, €30.8/m² for a one- or two-room flat, about €1,140 for 37 m², and a saturated market.

For whom? UN, WHO, ICRC, airport. For CERN, look at Saint-Genis-Pouilly first.

6. Thonon-les-Bains: the lake, the real budget, and still a train

At 35 km, Thonon is the town in this guide where a studio becomes affordable again: €18.9/m² for a one- or two-room flat, about €700 for 37 m², with the lake, a harbour and a centre that is alive all year round. The Léman Express runs there: Geneva Eaux-Vives in 43 minutes by train, every 30 minutes at rush hour, which makes 50 minutes door to door (2026 timetable). The CGN boat connects Lausanne, not Geneva.

For whom? Anyone trading close to an hour and a half on the train each day for a much lower rent and the lake.

7. Divonne-les-Bains: the upmarket end of the Pays de Gex

Casino, golf, spa, a lake, and the canton of Vaud next door. By public transport you need a bus to Coppet and then the train: Cornavin in 50 minutes door to door from the town hall (Monday, 8:30). One- or two-room asking rent: €30.3/m², about €1,120 for 37 m².

For whom? Families with a car, or anyone working in Nyon or near Coppet.

The other towns people ask us about

  • Saint-Genis-Pouilly: CERN's town. Cornavin in 45 minutes by bus then tram from the town hall (Monday, 8:30); €24.6/m² for a one- or two-room flat.
  • Gaillard and Étrembières: between Annemasse and the Moillesulaz crossing, tram 17 and buses; €23.0/m².
  • Évian-les-Bains: Geneva Eaux-Vives in 52 minutes by Léman Express; €21.2/m².
  • Bons-en-Chablais, Reignier-Ésery, La Roche-sur-Foron: Léman Express stations 25 to 35 minutes by train from Geneva Eaux-Vives, rents of €18 to €20/m², a train every 30 minutes.

What the town does not change: taxes, health insurance, permit

  • Taxes. As an employee in the canton of Geneva, you are taxed at source in Geneva whether you live in Haute-Savoie or in the Ain, then you file in France with no double taxation (our cross-border tax guide). The one exception: an employer in the canton of Vaud, and you pay in France.
  • Health insurance. LAMal or French insurance, the choice is made within three months of starting the job, whatever your town: LAMal or CMU, the comparison.
  • The G permit. Tied to your job, not your address, as long as you go home at least once a week: the G permit guide.

What really changes with the town: the rent, the commute and its cost, priced in the true cost of cross-border transport.

When it's not the right choice

If you go out late in Geneva several nights a week, the last train will remind you of the border: read our Switzerland-or-France comparison. If you arrive as a family with two cars, Vétraz-Monthoux, Cranves-Sales or the Pays de Gex beat central Annemasse. If your budget is really tight, the Chablais and the Arve valley win, provided you accept a train every 30 minutes. And if you are searching from afar, pay nothing before a visit: cross-border housing scams target exactly the people in a hurry.

Frequently asked questions

What is the closest French town to Geneva?

Gaillard and Ambilly touch the border at the Moillesulaz crossing, 5 km from the centre of Geneva, and central Annemasse is 8 km away. By train, Annemasse wins: Geneva Eaux-Vives in 7 minutes by Léman Express from the station.

Where should I live on the French side if I work at CERN?

In Saint-Genis-Pouilly, CERN's town, or in Ferney-Voltaire. By bus then tram, Cornavin is 45 minutes from Saint-Genis town hall. They are two of the most expensive towns on the border: €24.6 and €30.8/m² for a one- or two-room flat (Carte des loyers 2025).

Which is the cheapest border town near Geneva?

Among the towns in this guide, Ville-la-Grand (€17.1/m²) and Thonon-les-Bains (€18.9/m²) show the lowest one- or two-room asking rents (Carte des loyers, third quarter of 2025). Further out, Bons-en-Chablais and La Roche-sur-Foron drop below €19/m² while keeping a Léman Express station.

Can you live on the French side without a car?

Yes, in Annemasse, Ville-la-Grand and Ambilly: Léman Express every 10 minutes at rush hour, tram 17, the Voie Verte by bike. It is more constraining in Saint-Julien, Ferney or Saint-Genis (bus) and hard day to day in Divonne or Gex. Many of our residents do not own a car.

Haute-Savoie or Ain: what does it change?

For an employee of the canton of Geneva, nothing on taxes or health insurance. Haute-Savoie (Annemasse, Saint-Julien, Thonon) looks towards the left bank and the centre; the Ain (Ferney, Saint-Genis, Divonne) towards the airport, the international organisations and CERN. Pick your employer's side, not the other way round.

Which French towns are on the Swiss border, beyond Geneva?

Saint-Louis and Huningue opposite Basel, Pontarlier and Morteau opposite the canton of Neuchâtel and the Vaud Jura, Évian and Thonon opposite Lausanne across the lake. This guide only covers the Geneva basin, by far the largest.
