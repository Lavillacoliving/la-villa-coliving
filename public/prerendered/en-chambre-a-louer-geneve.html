Where to look for a room on the French side when you work in Geneva

The areas

Annemasse, Ambilly and Ville-la-Grand: Léman Express and tram 17, Geneva Eaux-Vives in 7 minutes by train from Annemasse station. Saint-Julien-en-Genevois: buses to Plan-les-Ouates and Carouge. Pays de Gex (Ferney-Voltaire, Saint-Genis): airport, UN, CERN.

Coliving operators on the French side

La Villa Coliving: 3 coliving houses (La Villa in Ville-la-Grand, Le Loft in Ambilly, Le Lodge in Annemasse), 29 furnished rooms of 16 to 24 m², pool, sauna and gym, all inclusive from CHF 1,290 to 1,430 a month. And L'Appart, a flatshare in Annemasse, near the station: 4 rooms of 12 to 22 m², with bills, fibre, streaming and cleaning of the shared areas included, from CHF 730 to 990 a month. In euros: €790 to €1,060 a month at L'Appart, €1,380 to €1,530 in the houses. No application fee, your employment contract is the file. Apply in 2 minutes, answer within 48 hours.

Peer-to-peer flatshare platforms

La Carte des Colocs, Leboncoin, Roomlala (service fees may apply to the tenant depending on the listing): rooms from €700 to €1,000 depending on the town, bills often on top.

Facebook groups

The public group "Coliving & Colocation à Genève et alentours !" (about 1,600 members, around a hundred listings a month) and cross-border commuter groups. The former is run and moderated by the La Villa Coliving team: published rules, suspicious listings removed.

The CAGI housing exchange

For employees of international organisations: the housing exchange of the Geneva Welcome Centre (CAGI) lists offers in Geneva, in the canton of Vaud and in neighbouring France.

Your file

No Swiss payslip yet? At La Villa Coliving the file comes down to three items: a signed employment contract or job offer, an ID, and a deposit of two months' rent excluding bills; a guarantor is only asked for case by case, when the contract does not cover the rent — we tell you before we reply, never after the visit. Elsewhere, prepare your contract, ID, proof of income and, often, a guarantor.

Price of a room to rent in Geneva

In Geneva itself, based on listings observed in 2026, a room usually rents for 1,000 to 1,500 CHF a month and a studio 1,800 to 2,500 CHF excluding charges, around 50 CHF/m² advertised according to our cross-border housing Observatory survey (June 2026); the vacancy rate is below 1% (OCSTAT). « Cheap room in Geneva »: compare the total cost, not the advertised rent. In Geneva you add bills, an internet subscription, cleaning and the furniture to buy to the bare rent; on the French side, at La Villa Coliving, the package from CHF 1,290/month includes everything: furnished room, bills, fibre, parking, cleaning of the common areas, pool, sauna, gym. Comfort and space make the difference, not the lowest rent.

Room in someone's home, sublet or coliving?

In someone's home

You live in the landlord's flat, by their rules, often without a real lease. Fine for a few weeks; hard to make a home.

Sublet

Your right to stay depends on someone else's lease. Bills on top, no guarantee of duration, and the main tenant between you and the owner.

Coliving at La Villa

A direct, furnished lease in your name, a single all-inclusive rent, a whole house shared with people who work in Geneva, and no landlord on site.
