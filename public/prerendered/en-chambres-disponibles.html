La Villa Coliving — the essentials

At La Villa Coliving, a furnished room in one of our three houses, in Ville-la-Grand, Ambilly and Annemasse, on the French side of the Geneva border, costs CHF 1,290 to 1,430 a month depending on the room, all-inclusive (the lease is in euros: €1,380 to €1,530).

That price covers everything you would pay separately elsewhere: utilities (water, electricity, heating), fibre internet, on-site parking, cleaning of the shared areas three times a week, the laundry room, everyday supplies delivered each month, bed linen and a towel on arrival, and six streaming services on the shared screen.

It also covers what a flatshare doesn't offer: a pool, a sauna, a gym and a home cinema in each house, yoga and fitness coaching classes, and a get-together every month.

Only your home insurance comes on top: no application fee, no agency fee.

Geneva benchmarks, November 2026: a room in a managed flatshare, furnished with all bills included, is listed at a median of CHF 1,570 a month (CHF 1,250 to 1,785); a room rented for CHF 1,000 from a private landlord comes to CHF 1,290–1,400 with running costs and a parking space, or CHF 1,090–1,200 without a car. Rooms and prices — frequently asked questions

La Villa Coliving runs three coliving houses on the French side, 20 min from Geneva Eaux-Vives by Léman Express, door to door: La Villa in Ville-la-Grand (10 residents), Le Loft in Ambilly (7 residents) and Le Lodge in Annemasse (12 residents). Each has a pool, a sauna, a gym and a home cinema space.

The three La Villa Coliving houses offer the same all-inclusive experience, at CHF 1,430/month (at La Villa, the 4 rooms with a shower room shared between 2 rooms, cleaning by our housekeeping team included, are CHF 1,370/month for rooms 3 and 4 and CHF 1,290/month for rooms 7 and 8). La Villa, in Ville-la-Grand, stands out for its large garden and quiet location; Le Loft, in Ambilly, for its indoor pool heated year-round; Le Lodge, in Annemasse, for its size and its fitness chalet. The choice depends on the atmosphere you're after and availability.

In La Villa Coliving's houses, your all-inclusive rent from CHF 1,290/month covers a furnished room of 16 to 24 m², all utilities, Orange Pro fibre up to 8 Gb/s, on-site parking, cleaning of common areas three times a week, access to the pool, sauna and gym, yoga and fitness classes and community events. At L'Appart, our flatshare in Annemasse, the rent from CHF 730/month covers the furnished room, bills, fibre, streaming and cleaning of the shared areas once a week; bed linen and cleaning products are not included. A single monthly payment, with no application fees and no hidden costs.

La Villa Coliving's rent is higher than a room in a standard flatshare, but not expensive for what it pays for. In our houses, CHF 1,290 to 1,430 a month pays for a 16 to 24 m² room and 38 to 49 m² of indoor space per person, the pool, the sauna, the gym, the home cinema, the large outdoor areas and what comes with them (barbecue, terraces). It also pays for cleaning of the shared areas three times a week, all maintenance, indoors and out, the monthly pizza party and the events, everyday supplies (toilet paper, laundry detergent, cleaning products), bills, fibre and streaming, with no application fees. In euros, the lease rent is €1,380 to €1,530. A standard flatshare has a lower rent, for a 9 to 12 m² room. But per square metre, it asks €25 to €45 for the rent alone, with no pool, no sauna, no gym and no cleaning, and bills on top. Here, it's €31 to €40 per m², with the pool, sauna, gym, cleaning and bills included. It's cheaper than it looks. And for a budget under €1,000 a month, there is L'Appart, our flatshare in Annemasse, near the station: rooms from €790, bills included. In Swiss francs: from CHF 730 a month.
