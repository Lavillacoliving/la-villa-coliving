La Villa Coliving — the essentials

La Villa Coliving is 3 coliving houses on the Geneva border, French side: La Villa in Ville-la-Grand (10 rooms), Le Loft in Ambilly (7 rooms) and Le Lodge in Annemasse (12 rooms) — 29 private furnished rooms of 16 to 24 m², with a pool, sauna and gym in every house, 20 minutes door-to-door from Geneva city centre.

  • All-inclusive rent from CHF 1,370/month (contractual rent in euros: from €1,470) — utilities, fibre up to 8 Gb/s, common-area cleaning 3 times a week, pool, sauna, gym, streaming, yoga and events included.
  • No application fee, no agency fee. Deposit: 2 months' rent, excluding charges.
  • 12-month lease. Minimum commitment of 3 months, then you're free to leave with 1 month's notice.
  • Commute: La Villa — Annemasse station 10 min on foot · central Geneva in 20 min door-to-door ; Le Loft — Annemasse station 10 min on foot · tram 17 at 5 min · central Geneva in 20 min door-to-door ; Le Lodge — Annemasse station 9 min on foot · central Geneva in 20 min door-to-door.
  • Who it's for: cross-border workers, expats and young professionals working in Geneva. Founded in October 2021 by Jérôme Austin and Fanny Bela and run directly by them — 100+ residents welcomed.

Rooms and prices — frequently asked questions

La Villa Coliving runs three coliving houses under 20 minutes from Geneva city center, on the French side: La Villa in Ville-la-Grand (10 residents), Le Loft in Ambilly (7 residents) and Le Lodge in Annemasse (12 residents). Each has a pool, a sauna, a gym and a home cinema space.

The three La Villa Coliving houses offer the same all-inclusive experience, at CHF 1,430/month (CHF 1,370/month for the 4 rooms at La Villa with a shower room shared between 2 rooms, cleaning by our housekeeping team included). La Villa, in Ville-la-Grand, stands out for its large garden and quiet location; Le Loft, in Ambilly, for its indoor pool heated year-round; Le Lodge, in Annemasse, for its size and its fitness chalet. The choice depends on the atmosphere you're after and availability.

At La Villa Coliving, your all-inclusive rent from CHF 1,370/month covers a furnished room of 17 to 25 m², all utilities, fibre internet up to 8 Gb/s, cleaning of common areas three times a week, access to the pool, sauna and gym, yoga and fitness classes and community events. A single monthly payment, with no application fees and no hidden costs.

Because you're not getting the same thing. Our rooms are 16-24 m² (vs 9-12 m² for most local flatshares), every house has a pool, sauna and gym, professional cleaning is included, a dedicated team maintains the house (gardener, pool technician), and community life is organised monthly. Per square metre and like-for-like on services, La Villa is at market price — all inclusive from CHF 1,370/month, with no application or agency fees.
