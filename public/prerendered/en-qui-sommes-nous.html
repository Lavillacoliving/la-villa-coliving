La Villa Coliving

Who we are

Two founders, three houses, one conviction: coliving is first and foremost about community. Since October 2021, Jérôme and Fanny have run their Greater Geneva houses, on the French side, directly — no agency between you and us.

The story, in brief

October 2021: the first house opened in Ville-la-Grand, with a simple conviction — a coliving should be a living space designed around its community, not just an upgraded flatshare. Today, La Villa Coliving brings together 3 houses — La Villa in Ville-la-Grand, Le Loft in Ambilly, Le Lodge in Annemasse — 29 furnished rooms, all-inclusive from CHF 1,370/month, 20 minutes from central Geneva.

More than 100 residents have lived there since: cross-border workers and expats arriving in the Geneva basin, most between 25 and 40. And renting is direct — the people who own and look after the houses are the ones you talk to, from the first message to the key handover. No agency, no middleman: us.

Our values

Community, our reason for being

We don't rent out rooms, we bring houses to life: a community dinner every month, yoga and sport every week, events all year round. Selecting housemates is only the first chapter — what happens next is what makes La Villa.

Not a factory

A stone's throw from Geneva, the new mega-residences pack up to 776 rooms — with less than 4 m² of communal space per resident. With us: 7 to 12 housemates per house, 37 to 42 m² of living space each, and whole gardens to share. You don't build a community in a building lobby.

Quality, no compromise

Designer furniture, pool, sauna, gym — real amenities, maintained as if we lived there. Because we're there, every week.

Direct and fully transparent

One price, all inclusive: no application fees, no agency fees, no surprises. And no hotline — us, directly reachable, with an answer within 48 hours.
