Frequently asked questions

How much does a room at L'Appart cost?
CHF 730 a month for each of the three 12 m² rooms, CHF 990 a month for the 22 m² suite. In euros, the rent on the lease: €790 and €1,060. The rent covers bills (water, electricity, heating), fibre, streaming and cleaning of the shared areas once a week (shared shower room and toilet included). No application fee.
What does the rent include, and what doesn't it?
Included: bills (water, electricity, heating), fibre, streaming, cleaning of the shared areas once a week (shared shower room and toilet included), and access to the yoga and fitness classes and the social evenings held at the houses. Not included: bed linen and towels, cleaning products and household supplies, parking (on request) and your home insurance.
Does L'Appart have a pool?
No. L'Appart has no pool, sauna or gym: it's a flatshare in a flat, not a coliving house. You do have access to the yoga and fitness classes held at La Villa Coliving's houses. And if you want a pool and a sauna at home, that's the houses, from CHF 1,290 a month.
What's the difference between L'Appart and La Villa Coliving's houses?
The houses are colivings: 7 to 12 housemates, rooms of 16 to 24 m², a pool, a sauna and a gym in every house, shared areas cleaned three times a week, from CHF 1,290 a month. L'Appart is a flatshare: 4 rooms in a 151 m² flat near Annemasse station, rooms of 12 to 22 m², an 80 m² living room and kitchen and a balcony, shared areas cleaned once a week (shared shower room and toilet included), from CHF 730 a month. Same founders, same lease, same deposit, and no application fee either way.
Can two people rent together?
Yes, in the suite: it's the only room at La Villa Coliving let to one or two people. It's 22 m², with its own shower room and toilet, a private balcony and its own door onto the landing. The rent is €1,060 a month, or €530 each for two. The bed is 140 cm wide, as in the other rooms. The three 12 m² rooms are for one person each.
When can I move in to L'Appart?
L'Appart opens for rent in February 2027. You can apply now to reserve a room or the suite: the application takes 2 minutes and we reply within 48 hours.
How long does it take to get to Geneva from L'Appart?
Annemasse station is a 5-minute walk away. From there the Léman Express reaches Geneva Eaux-Vives in 7 minutes and Cornavin in 23, with no change. Door to door, allow 13 minutes to Geneva Eaux-Vives, 22 minutes to Rive, in the city centre, and 29 minutes to Cornavin. Tram 17 is a 2-minute walk away, at the Place Deffaugt stop.
What are the terms for renting at L'Appart?
The same as in La Villa Coliving's houses. No set minimum: your lease runs 12 months and you can leave whenever you want with 1 month's notice. Deposit of 2 months' rent excluding charges, payable in 1, 2 or 3 instalments depending on your application, no application or agency fee. On guarantors, one rule: a guarantor is only asked for case by case, when the contract does not cover the rent — we tell you before we reply, never after the visit. Students and apprentices welcome.

Want a pool and a sauna?

That's our three coliving houses: La Villa in Ville-la-Grand, Le Loft in Ambilly, Le Lodge in Annemasse. A pool, a sauna and a gym in each, rooms of 16 to 24 m², from CHF 1,290 a month.
