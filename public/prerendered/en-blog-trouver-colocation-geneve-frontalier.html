Finding Shared Housing in Geneva (2026)

La Villa TeamMay 24, 2026· Updated August 31, 20266 min

Finding a colocation near Geneva when you work in Switzerland is a particular journey: high Swiss-side prices, a tight market, French-style applications. Here's how to go about it, step by step, without wasting time or getting scammed.

Shared housing in Geneva or on the French side?

First decision, and it's quickly settled for most cross-border workers. In Geneva itself, rents are among Europe's highest and supply is scarce (vacancy rate below 1%). As a result, the vast majority of "Geneva colocations" are actually on the French side, in Annemasse, Ville-la-Grand or Ambilly — 20 minutes from the center by Léman Express, for rents 30 to 50% lower. It's the best budget/commute trade-off. To pick your town, see our guide to the best cross-border neighborhoods.

Where to actually look?

Several options, each with pros and cons:

  • cross-border Facebook groups (very active, but first come first served);
  • listings sites (leboncoin, etc.) — many offers, but filter carefully;
  • French real estate agencies (serious, but application fees and heavy files);
  • coliving operators, who handle everything end to end (furnished room, utilities, community).

How to avoid scams?

The cross-border market unfortunately attracts fraudulent listings (fake room, deposit requested before a viewing…). Golden rule: never send money before viewing and signing. We detail every trap in our cross-border housing scams guide.

What budget to plan?

A room in shared housing on the French side generally runs from €600 to €900 in a classic rental (excluding utilities), or around 1,370 CHF all-inclusive in premium coliving. To frame your budget precisely by salary, see how much rent as a cross-border worker and our pricing page.

What application for shared housing on the French side?

In a classic rental, expect to provide "French-style" documents (income, sometimes a guarantor) — not always simple when you're paid in Switzerland. The details are in our cross-border rental application guide. Good news: in coliving, the application is simplified — no French guarantor required.
