## What we'll never ask of you

1. **No payment before the lease is signed.** No deposit, no "booking fee", no down payment "to hold the room". If someone asks you for money before you've signed, it isn't us.
2. **A viewing before any commitment.** On site with someone from our team, or by live video if you're far away. You see the room, the house and the person showing you around.
3. **A completely standard furnished-rental lease**, signed by both sides, with an address, a company and named contacts. Nothing unusual, nothing hidden.

One detail says it all: fake landlords almost always ask for a bank transfer before the viewing. It's the most reliable warning sign there is. We do the opposite.

## The scams we see around Annemasse and Geneva

The cross-border market is tight, people search fast, often from far away, and that attracts scammers. Here are the four patterns we come across most:

- **The transfer before the viewing.** You have to "hold" the room with a deposit or an application fee before you've seen it. Once the money's gone, so is everyone.
- **The landlord abroad.** They can't show you around, but they'll post you the keys as soon as you've paid the first month. There are no keys, and no flat.
- **Stolen photos.** Do a reverse image search: if the same room shows up elsewhere, at another address or under another name, walk away. And if you see our houses under a name other than ours, tell us.
- **Pressure.** "Three other people are interested, you need to decide tonight." A good landlord gives you time to think — at least a night.

**The right reflex, in one sentence**: no viewing, no verifiable identity, no lease → no money. In that order, no exceptions.
